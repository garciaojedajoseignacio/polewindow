PoleWindow

Compare lunar south-pole landing sites and date ranges. See when the Sun is above the horizon (solar-power window) and when Earth is above the horizon (direct-to-Earth communications window).

Limitation: elevations use a smooth spherical lunar horizon. Local LOLA topography (crater walls and ridgelines) is not applied, so a low Sun or Earth may be hidden in reality. Crater-floor centers will look more sunlit than they really are.
